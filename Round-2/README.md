3. Quantitative comparison reported in the manuscript
The revised manuscript reports an expert-rated comparison of four approaches:
- Ward & Peppard
- TOGAF ADM
- COBIT 2019
- PRASASTI
According to the manuscript, the same 19 respondents rated the four approaches against nine common criteria using a five-point scale. Criterion-level means, normalized aggregate scores, and paired-samples t-test results are reported in Tables 11–13.
Approach	Reported normalized score
Ward & Peppard	54%
TOGAF ADM	61%
COBIT 2019	66%
PRASASTI	81%


These are expert ratings of framework approaches. They should not be interpreted as measured improvements in organizational performance or as results from a real-world implementation study.

The manuscript also reports a mean expert-assessed applicability score of 71% across eight PRASASTI dimensions, compared with a study-defined evaluation benchmark of 85%. The reported average gap is 14 percentage points. The benchmark is not presented as a universal maturity standard or as an organizational-readiness score.
4. Interpretation and reproducibility notes
1. Unit of analysis: The applicability index concerns expert-assessed applicability and implementation feasibility of the PRASASTI framework artifact. It does not measure the readiness or maturity of a specific organization.
2. Comparison scope: The quantitative comparison uses expert ratings across common criteria. It is not a controlled, same-organization implementation comparison and does not establish improvements in planning time, implementation success, or organizational outcomes.
3. Benchmark: The 85% value is a study-defined evaluation benchmark. Sensitivity analysis at alternative benchmarks is descriptive and does not independently validate the benchmark.
4. Rounding: The manuscript states that respondent percentages are rounded to the nearest whole number for presentation. Statistical calculations should use the underlying numerical values, as documented in the manuscript and workbook.
5. Percentage-point gaps: Evaluation benchmark (%) − current score (%) is expressed in percentage points (pp). For example, 85% − 78% = 7 percentage points, not a relative percentage deficit.
6. Missing values and exclusions: Consult the workbook and its notes for documented handling of missing values, exclusions, and intermediate calculations. Do not assume a rule that is not documented in the available materials.
7. Expert-validation safeguards: The manuscript acknowledges that evaluator anonymity, framework-developer participation, and agreement by expert category may not be fully documented. Treat these as limitations unless they can be confirmed from original study records.
8. Data protection: Respondent-related materials should be anonymized and shared publicly only where ethically and legally permissible. Do not include direct identifiers or confidential information.

6. Scope of the available materials
This repository is intended to make the revised manuscript and available supporting materials easier to inspect. The degree of independent reproduction depends on which instruments, anonymized respondent-level data, Delphi-round records, data dictionaries, and calculation details are actually included in the deposited files.

Do not infer that a complete questionnaire, Delphi revision log, full item-level ratings, or executable scripts are available unless those materials are present. Where materials cannot be shared for confidentiality or research-ethics reasons, the manuscript should state the restriction and provide an auditable substitute where feasible.
7. Suggested review workflow
1. Open the revised manuscript and note its current section, table, and figure numbering.
2. Open the response letter and locate the comment being assessed.
3. Follow the response's stated location into the manuscript.
